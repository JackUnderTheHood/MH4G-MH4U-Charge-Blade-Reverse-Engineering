## Why was this file not polished directly?

`current_state.md` is a live research record, not a finished document. It was appended as experiments progressed and contains:

- hypotheses that were still awaiting verification at the time;
- failed and later-withdrawn candidates;
- older interpretations corrected by later evidence;
- installation, readback, restoration, and next-step records;
- temporary stop conditions written for experimental safety.

Rewriting it would damage the research history and make it harder to determine when a conclusion was superseded. It is therefore preserved unchanged and used only as a Research Archive.

Public reading should begin with the repository README, followed by Technical Analysis, Research History, and Porting Notes as needed.
